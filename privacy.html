Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Sample text for the template. Replace it with copy reviewed by your own legal adviser.

1. Information we collect

We collect the details you give us when you create an account, take an order or make a reservation, such as your name, email address, phone number and order history.

2. How we use it

We use your information to run the restaurant, process payments, confirm bookings, improve our menu and, with your consent, send offers.

3. Sharing

We share data only with providers who help us operate, such as payment processors and delivery partners. We never sell personal information.

4. Retention and security

We keep records for as long as needed for accounting and legal reasons, and protect them with access controls and encrypted connections.

5. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data at any time by contacting support@foodtown.example.com.
